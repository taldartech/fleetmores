CLEAR ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TruckBill deployment, accounting, data security, and daily transport workflows.

FleetMores is the transportation technology company behind TruckBill. TruckBill is our dedicated cloud ERP platform purpose-built for Indian transporters, fleet owners, commission brokers, and logistics agencies to handle trips, bilties, freight invoices, TDS compliance, and receivables in one place.

Yes, exactly. TruckBill was engineered specifically for hybrid Indian operations. For owned trucks, it tracks fuel debits, vehicle fitness, and maintenance; for market hired trucks, it creates Lorry Hire Slips (LHS), deducts broker commissions, and manages settlement balances with vehicle owners.

TruckBill automatically calculates Section 194C TDS (1% for individuals/HUF contractors, 2% for corporate entities) during freight bill generation. It tracks customer deductions directly against Form 26AS so your Chartered Accountant has zero reconciliation headaches at financial year-end.

Standard deployment takes less than 48 hours. Our onboarding team provides pre-formatted Excel templates to import your historical customer master lists, vehicle lists, driver profiles, and branch locations directly into your new instance.

Completely secure. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256 cloud encryption. Automated daily backups ensure your records are never lost, and strict branch-level role controls prevent unauthorized staff from viewing sensitive company financials.
