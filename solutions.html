  • ✓ Multi-LR Monthly Invoicing: Combine hundreds of dispatches into a clean single tax invoice.
  • ✓ Detention & Unloading Billing: Automatically append loading/unloading detention slips to final bills.

SOLUTION 02

For Fleet Owners (Own Truck Operators)

When you own vehicles, profit is won or lost in diesel management, driver trip advances, tire life, and vehicle turnaround times.

  • ✓ Vehicle-Level P&L: Track exact revenue and all trip expenses per truck every month.
  • ✓ Driver Advance vs Expense Audit: Reconcile driver cash and pump slips before closing the trip.
  • ✓ Diesel Fuel Mileage Audit: Detect route fuel leakage and underperforming drivers instantly.
  • ✓ Document Expiry Tracker: Never pay fines for expired National Permits, Fitness, or Insurance.

SOLUTION 03

For Commission Agents & Freight Brokers

Hiring market vehicles demands instant rate locks, advance diesel slip management, commission deduction tracking, and tight supplier reconciliation.

  • ✓ Dual-Rate Lorry Vouchers: Log party freight vs truck owner hire rate in one simple entry.
  • ✓ Commission Margin Safeguard: Instantly calculate gross and net brokerage margins per trip.
  • ✓ WhatsApp Payment Slips: Send instant balance memos and settlement receipts to truck owners.
  • ✓ Supplier TDS Compliance: Full recordkeeping for 194C non-deduction declarations (PAN 10-truck rule).
